Is Watershed free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT or Apache-2.0 licence, with no account and no subscription.

Does Watershed upload my songs?

No. Songs are split on your own computer. The only download is the model, once.

Can I open stems from other tools?

Yes. Drop a folder of stems, for example from MVSEP, and it opens in the player, no graphics card needed.

Does Watershed replace my DAW?

No, it plays beside it. Your instrument stays in Reaper or any other DAW; Watershed plays the band.
